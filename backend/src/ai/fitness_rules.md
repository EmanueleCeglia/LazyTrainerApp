# LazyTrainer Fitness Rulebook

This document contains the strict guidelines, split preferences, and training methodologies that the Strategist AI MUST follow when designing a workout skeleton. These rules are sourced from top fitness experts (Gymshark & Gainful).

## 1. Choosing the Right Workout Split (Based on Experience & Days)

The AI must select the appropriate workout split based on the user's experience level and how many days per week they can train.

*   **Beginners (2-3 Days/Week) -> Full Body Split**
    *   *Structure:* Monday: Full Body, Wednesday: Full Body, Friday: Full Body (Always allow 48 hours rest between sessions).
    *   *Focus:* Hits all major muscles each session. Prioritize compound exercises to build a foundation.
*   **Intermediate (3 or 6 Days/Week) -> Push, Pull, Legs (PPL)**
    *   *Structure:* Day 1: Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps), Day 2: Pull (Back, Biceps), Day 3: Legs (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves). 
    *   *Focus:* Good balance of compound and isolation movements.
*   **Advanced or Strength-Focused (4 Days/Week) -> Upper / Lower Split**
    *   *Structure:* Upper, Lower, Rest, Upper, Lower, Rest.
    *   *Focus:* The best split for building pure strength and testing limits on heavy compound lifts. Hits muscles twice a week.
*   **Advanced or Hypertrophy-Focused (5 Days/Week) -> Body Part Split (Bro Split)**
    *   *Structure:* E.g., Chest/Biceps, Quads/Glutes, Rest, Back/Triceps, Hamstrings/Glutes, Shoulders/Traps.
    *   *Focus:* Maximum volume for single muscle groups. Prioritizes isolation exercises. Ideal for bodybuilders wanting to grow specific muscles.

## 2. Choosing the Right Training Methodology (Based on Goals)

The AI must assign specific sets, reps, and methods based on the user's primary goal.

*   **Goal: Muscle & Strength (Hypertrophy / Power)**
    *   *Method:* Resistance training utilizing the "Big Five" (Squats, Deadlifts, Bench Press, Barbell Row, Overhead Press).
    *   *Metrics:* 4 sets of 6-8 reps. Heavy weights.
    *   *Focus:* Progressive overload.
*   **Goal: Weight Loss (Fat Loss)**
    *   *Method:* High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) and metabolic conditioning mixed with weight training. 
    *   *Metrics:* Fast-paced. Keep heart rate elevated. 
    *   *Focus:* 90-100% maximal capacity during bursts, short rests.
*   **Goal: Weight Gain / Bulking**
    *   *Method:* Powerlifting style. Very heavy weights, low reps. 
    *   *Metrics:* 3-5 reps per set. Long rests to ensure maximum power output.
*   **Goal: Maintenance / Toning**
    *   *Method:* General physical activity and lighter resistance training.
    *   *Metrics:* Lower-weight, higher-rep sets (e.g., 12-15 reps).
